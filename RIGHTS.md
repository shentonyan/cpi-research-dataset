# Rights, attribution and local full-text use

Read the full Chinese [project declaration](DISCLAIMER.md). This is an independent scholarly index, not an official publisher, university or author service. Public availability is not blanket permission to copy third-party works.

## Scope of rights

Third-party articles, abstracts, figures and datasets remain subject to their respective rights, licenses and access terms. The project claims no ownership of those materials and grants no MIT, CC0, CC BY or other blanket license over them. Registry license fields must be checked for the specific object and version; conflicting statements do not establish full-text redistribution permission.

Unless a specific file states otherwise, no general reuse license is granted for project-authored code or prose either. A public GitHub repository is not automatically an open-source license. Platform rights to view and fork are separate from broader reuse rights. Attribution and source links do not themselves create permission to republish a work.

## Full text for research and writing

Researchers may obtain PDFs through lawful and authorized channels and use them within applicable permission, contractual and legal limits. A research purpose or this declaration does not itself authorize acquisition, redistribution or uploading to third-party AI services. Do not bypass access controls or embargoes.

PDFs, OCR, extracted full text, raw abstract caches, private notes and restricted data are local-only by default. They are not included in the public website, repository or public packages. Public records retain bibliographic facts, source links, provenance, screening decisions and rule-derived themes; third-party original abstract text is omitted where redistribution rights have not been established.

## Research accountability

AI-assisted screening, thematic tags and map edges are aids, not full-text review, independent replication, legal clearance or endorsement. Verify original sources and cite accurately. Copyright and academic-integrity obligations do not disappear after paraphrasing or AI processing.

## Source guidance

- [Crossref metadata retrieval](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/): most metadata is reusable without restriction, while abstracts may be subject to publisher or author copyright.
- [GitHub repository licensing](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository): making a repository public does not itself grant a general reuse license.
- [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/legalcode.en): the applicable license, work, version and attribution conditions matter; this repository does not replace them.

Report a correction or rights concern via [Issues](https://github.com/shentonyan/cpi-research-dataset/issues), identifying the record, location, concern and supporting basis.
