# Contributing research records

Please contribute bibliographic corrections, DOI/source links, independently written notes, and reproducible methods. Distinguish your own analysis from an author's claims and cite the specific source.

Do not upload paper PDFs, scanned pages, OCR, extracted full text, bulk original abstracts, confidential data, private notes, credentials or non-public manuscripts. Keep local research material under ignored paths. A research purpose and a copyright notice do not themselves authorize redistribution.

For any separately licensed third-party contribution, identify the precise work/version, source, applicable permission and attribution. Do not apply a project-wide license to third-party works. Rights concerns can be reported through Issues without uploading the disputed full text.

Before proposing a change, run `python scripts/validate_public.py`; for map data also run `python scripts/validate_map.py`. Follow [the declaration](DISCLAIMER.md) and [local PDF workflow](docs/pdf-workflow.zh-CN.md).
